Nutritional Guidelines

Evidence-based guidelines that form the foundation of your BLU longevity plan. Click any card to expand.

Dietary fiber feeds your gut microbiome, regulates blood sugar, lowers LDL cholesterol, and reduces systemic inflammation — cornerstones of a long healthspan.
Today → Add 2 tbsp ground flaxseed (+6g), lentils at lunch (+16g), avocado at dinner (+10g).
Plant proteins come bundled with fiber and phytonutrients. Aim for 0.7–1g per pound of bodyweight daily, with plants leading every meal.
Today → Swap ground beef for lentils — same satiety, triple the fiber.
Omega-3s reduce cellular inflammation and protect cardiovascular health. Trans fats accelerate biological aging.
Today → Drizzle walnut oil on your salad and add chia seeds to yogurt.
Proper hydration supports every cellular function. Even mild dehydration impairs cognition and energy.
Today → 16oz of water before coffee — rehydrates after sleep.
Your gut microbiome is directly linked to immune function, mood, metabolism, and longevity.
Today → Add kimchi to lunch or stir miso into your salad dressing.
Time-restricted eating activates autophagy — your body's cellular clean-up process — and improves metabolic health.
Today → First meal at 8am → finish eating by 6pm.
